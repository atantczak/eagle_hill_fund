How to use this. Check it after the close. The rules read daily closes; during the session the page re-pulls the day's partial bar every 15 minutes (or now, with Refresh), and the vendor's end-of-day history runs a few minutes behind the tape, so an intraday reading is provisional until 4 pm ET. Act on what it says, including the false alarms: the record above includes every one of them. It reads closing prices, so it cannot protect against an overnight gap, and most years it will trail the market by a few points. That is the premium on the insurance.
